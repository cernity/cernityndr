Scaling:
  - Each detector is a Deployment; those with hpa.enabled also get a CPU HPA.
  - The real scaling signal is Kafka consumer-group LAG, not CPU. For lag-based
    autoscaling, install KEDA and add a kafka ScaledObject per detector Deployment
    targeting its consumer group (ndr-<detector>). CPU HPA is the built-in default.
  - Parallelism ceiling = topic partitions. Give the high-volume topics
    (suricata.flow.v1, dns, tls, http) enough partitions for your peak replica count.

Bring your own clustered bus/state/storage (Redpanda, Redis Cluster, ClickHouse);
this chart runs the stateless Cernity tier on top of them.

Check status:
  kubectl get deploy,hpa -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
